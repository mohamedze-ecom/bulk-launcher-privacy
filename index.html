Privacy Policy

Bulk Launcher · Effective October 25, 2026

Bulk Launcher is a private, internal tool used by its owner and their team to upload ad creatives and create ads in their own Meta (Facebook) ad accounts. This policy explains what information the tool handles when you log in with Facebook and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you choose “Continue with Facebook” and approve access, Bulk Launcher receives from Meta:

  • Your Facebook user ID, name and profile picture.
  • An access token that lets the tool act on your behalf within the permissions you approved.
  • The ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, Facebook Pages and linked Instagram accounts you have access to.

The tool also stores the settings you choose inside it: which ad accounts you connect and any launch presets you save (for example ad copy, links and naming templates).

Images and videos you upload are held temporarily only long enough to send them to Meta, and are deleted from the tool’s server as soon as each upload finishes.

How we use it

Information is used only to provide the tool’s features: showing your ad accounts and Pages, uploading your creatives to your ad accounts, and creating ad creatives, ad sets and ads exactly as you instruct. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Data is sent only to Meta, through Meta’s official Marketing API, to carry out the actions you request.

Storage and security

Your access token, connected accounts and presets are stored on the server that runs Bulk Launcher and are not shared with third parties. Access tokens expire automatically (typically after about 60 days). Logging in is limited to people who have been given a role on the tool.

Your choices and data deletion

  • You can disconnect any ad account inside the tool at any time.
  • You can remove Bulk Launcher’s access from your Facebook account under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) and removing “Bulk Launcher”. This immediately invalidates the access token.
  • To have all information about you deleted from the tool, email the address below with the subject “Delete my data”. We will delete your stored profile, token, connected accounts and presets within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

Bulk Launcher is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
